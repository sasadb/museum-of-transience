Tracks of Return

Digital capture of e-tickets (personal details obscured), 2026

A collection of train tickets, mostly Mugunghwa rather than KTX, reflecting the slow pace of a student's journey. While Mugunghwa, ITX, and KTX fares remain within reach, traveling through the Korean countryside often prompts quiet comparisons to childhood train journeys across Java—from home to the capital in an executive carriage.

Here, every ticket marks a destination, yet quietly traces a loop back to that initial platform. In memory, a station is never just a departure point; it is where one departs, arrives, and continuously returns.
